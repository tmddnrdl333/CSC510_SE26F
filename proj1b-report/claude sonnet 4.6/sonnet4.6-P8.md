**Candidate A**

Food delivery apps have turned ordering into a game, but a game nobody plays together — every streak, badge, and reward lives in a private scoreboard with an audience of one. Our app connects customers, restaurants, and delivery partners around a shared competitive layer that tracks loyalty points, achievement badges, and real charitable impact in a single persistent ranking. For the first time, users can see exactly where they stand against the friends they chose — not a seasonal promotion or a regional pilot, but a permanent friends-only leaderboard tied to every order. Restaurants gain a measurable link between their rewards programs and customer retention, and delivery partners become visible contributors to a community outcome, not just route-closers. When ordering feels like something you do *with* people rather than for yourself, everyone at the table orders more.

**Candidate B**

Gamification in food delivery stops at the individual: Starbucks tracks your stars, DoorDash tracks your Dash Cash, but neither tells you whether your roommate is beating you. We are building the first delivery app where loyalty points, earned badges, and donated meals — counted together, weighted equally — determine your rank among the friends you actually know. Our "Meal-for-a-Meal" donation counter links every order directly to a meal funded for someone in need, making charitable giving a ranked competitive dimension rather than a footnote. Friends see one honest leaderboard; restaurants see which loyalty mechanics actually move repeat orders; delivery partners see their volume translated into community impact they can point to. Competition with friends, grounded in real generosity, turns a transactional habit into something worth talking about at dinner.

**Candidate C**

Delivery apps have taught customers to chase rewards but left them with no one to chase them against — the leaderboard is missing its other players. We are fixing that by ranking friends across three dimensions at once: loyalty points earned, achievement badges unlocked, and meals donated through our Meal-for-a-Meal program, where each completed delivery funds a meal for a person facing food insecurity. Unlike Chipotle's Summer of Extras, which ended August 31, 2026 and never let you add a single friend, our leaderboard is permanent, personal, and built on verified identity so the scores mean something. Restaurants get a direct signal connecting their reward structures to repeat purchase behavior, and delivery partners earn a visible role in a ranked social outcome tied to their own work. The result is an app where the best reason to order tonight is knowing your friend ordered last night — and is currently winning.
