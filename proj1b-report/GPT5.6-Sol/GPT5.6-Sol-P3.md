I treat “every rival” as the major multi-sided delivery platforms—principally Uber Eats, DoorDash, Grubhub, Just Eat/Deliveroo, and Swiggy—not every local ordering app. Rare does not necessarily mean valuable: UC11 is distinctive, but considerably weaker strategically than UC19 or UC20.

## Proposed next-version use cases

| Proposed use case | Classification | Competitive assessment and one-month judgment |
|---|---|---|
| (a) AI assistant that personalizes the menu | DIFFERENTIATOR, narrowly | Basic algorithmic personalization is already table stakes at [Uber Eats](https://www.uber.com/us/en/blog/improving-uber-eats-home-feed-recommendations/), but an explicit assistant that interprets intent and constructs personalized choices is still emerging through [DoorDash Ask](https://about.doordash.com/en-us/news/ask-doordash), [Swiggy WhatToEat](https://blog.swiggy.com/news/swiggys-industry-first-whattoeat-feature-helps-users-order-food-attuned-to-their-moods-and-cravings/), and Just Eat; no confirmed major rival has abandoned a directly comparable assistant. |
| (b) Voice assistant that places and modifies orders | DIFFERENTIATOR, with an abandonment warning | [Alexa+](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/alexa-plus-grubhub-uber-eats-food-delivery) and [Just Eat](https://newsroom.justeattakeaway.com/en-GB/260037-what-do-you-want-for-dinner-just-eat-launches-ai-voice-assistant-to-help-uk-customers-decide/) show that transactional voice ordering is live, but McDonald’s ended its IBM automated voice-ordering trial after accuracy problems, demonstrating the testing and exception-handling risk. [Abandonment evidence](https://apnews.com/article/bebc898363f2d550e1a0cd3c682fa234) |

For a one-month build-and-test window, choose (a), but scope it to explainable recommendation and menu ranking using existing data—order history, cuisine, price, time, location, and ratings. Do not attempt a fully autonomous voice-ordering system in that period; the defensible smaller experiment would allow voice to add or remove pre-checkout cart items, then require visual confirmation before placing the order.
