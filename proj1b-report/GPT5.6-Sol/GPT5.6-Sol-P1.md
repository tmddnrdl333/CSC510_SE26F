Uber Eats did not make the ten: its current Merchant Rewards feature tracks personal progress—for example, five orders at one merchant to earn a discount—but I found no customer leaderboard, friend competition, badges, or impact ranking. That makes it less comparable than the products above. [Uber Eats Merchant Rewards](https://help.uber.com/en/ubereats/restaurants/article/earn-restaurant-rewards?nodeId=982b0c15-f8ed-4079-afef-d92feff52af9)

For a one-month build-and-test cycle, the defensible scope is an opt-in, weekly friends leaderboard over Hungry Wolf’s existing events: one composite score, “your rank,” nearby competitors, aliases/privacy controls, and a weekly reset. National rankings, cash prizes, custom games, contact syncing, and new donation-accounting infrastructure should wait; the closest precedents show that the social ranking layer itself is enough for a meaningful test.
