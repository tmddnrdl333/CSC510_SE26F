The most realistic one-month competitive target is not Alexa+–level autonomy. It is a constrained assistant resembling DoorDash Ask or Swiggy WhatToEat: personalized recommendations plus conversational cart construction, with an explicit visual confirmation before checkout. Kea also shows that transactional voice can be tested quickly when narrowly scoped to a structured menu and an existing order/POS API.
